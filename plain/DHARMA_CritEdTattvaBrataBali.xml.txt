[.1]i śaka, 1722. vai, ka, vrә, vāra marakih, pratipāda riṅ śukla pakṣa, asuji maśa, rah, 2, tǝṅgǝk, 2. i rika divasanya, pūrṇa linikhita. 
    translation : 
 [.1] Om̐. Let there be no obstacle.
 [.2]This is the Right Behaviour  in [Performing] Observances. When you perform an observance, do not do it haphazardly, without having permission to the one who is in charge of the observances, [namely] the reverend Kaki Citragopta [and] Nini Citragopta . [The request] should be received [by means of] an offering of a white and green betel leaf on a well. The mantra is: “My Lord Kaki Citragopta and Nini Citragopta, I ask permission to perform an observance. My Lord, please accompany me when I perform an observance! Everything that I strive after, let it be obtained! If I make a mistake when performing an observance, let it be blamed on the reverend one who is in charge of the observances!” All your observances [will then be] like that. Further, the teeth-polishing leaves [have to be used] by the one who carries out the observances. The exceedingly good one is the hambulu; the result is [to become] rich in gold. There are the teeth-polishing leaves of the kayәn ; the result is many children. There are the teeth-polishing leaves of the vaṅkal ; the result is [to become] rich in rice. There are the teeth-polishing leaves of the lampәni; the result is that you are successful. The dosage of all the mixtures are the same. It is appropriate to pay full attention to it. 
 [.3]This is the Holy Scripture  on the Observances. Perfection in observances, its reality is bodiless. The Holy Dharma, its reality is [like] bars of hedge, for it serves as the tie with the reality of the observances [which keeps them together], and [it] also releases supernatural power. They (i.e., the observances and the Dharma) are just like the left and right bodies. The reality of observances and asceticism is likewise. That is why, one who strives to obtain goodness should perform [observances and asceticism]. [If] young people perform observances, they will obtain the result of them at a young age. There is nothing that comes into being unless it is performed; there is nothing to be taken from the arrangement [according to] what has been arranged. As if one cooks rice in the morning and eats it that morning, one cooks rice at noon and eats it that noon, one cooks rice in the evening and eats in that evening. If one is not cooking, one is not eating. [The conclusion is that] the results are according to one’s conduct. Such is the right order of [actions and results]. Because the result of bad and good conduct is obtained, that is why good conduct should be performed without delay. [This] originates from the Holy Scripture on Observances. 
 [.4]This is The Detailed Enumeration of the Observances. It should be known.
 [.5]There is the observance of ‘Cutting Off the Taste’ (Mәgat Rasa): if one eats half [portion], and stands up  for chewing betel. It is fine if one does not speak. After chewing betel, one can eat again. Its appointed duration is one year. The deity is the Goddess Umā. The mantra is: “Om̐ Mam̐ hail to Umā!”. [I take] leave. Its result is that the love of husband and wife is not cut off, and all people love [you].
 [.6]There is the observance of eating rice from slaṅgi Its duration is seven months. The deity is the Goddess Vastu. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to the Goddess Vastu!” [I take ] leave. Its result is that the love of husband and wife is not cut off, and all people love you and feel sympathy for you. 
 [.7] There is the observance of the ‘Earth-Plate’ (Bhūmipattra): if one eats without using plates, and lays [the food] openly on earth. The deity is reverend Vaiśravaṇa. Its appointed duration is one year and seven months. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to the illustrious Vaiśravaṇa!” [I take ] leave. Its result is being rich in land and freeholds, and you are loved by the king and priests. 
 [.8]There is the observance of ‘Craving to Embroider’ (Anulam Iḍam): one fast all day, until the end of the day. Its appointed duration is three months. Its result is that all desires will be obtained quickly because of that. You will be beloved by men. The deity is the Reverend Brahmā. Mantra: “Om̐ Am̐ hail to Brahmā!” [I take] leave.
 [.9]There is the observance of eating before the Reverend Sun goes down. Its appointed duration is three years. The deity of the observance is the Lord Śiva. Its result is that the mind will be clear. Your thoughts are alive  in one goal. Mantra: “Om̐ Mam̐ hail to Śiva!” If you apply make up. 
 [.10]There is the observance of not eating when the Reverend Sun is already at sunset. Its appointed duration is one year. The deity is the Reverend Śiva. The mantra is: “Om̐, hail to Śiva!” [I take] leave. Its result is that one will be knowledgeable in every kind of work, especially in learning; and one will not be ignorant in playing the gamәlan , telling a story, and [composing] kakavin ; one will be handsome without equal. 
 [.11]There is the observance of ‘Full Moon’ (Amūrṇama): you are fasting every full moon. Its appointed duration is seven years and seven months. The deity is the Goddess Ratih. Mantra: “Om̐ hail to Śrī Ratih!” [I take] leave. Its result is that your work will be successful, God and Demon will love you. 
 [.12]There is the observance of ‘Dark Moon’ (Nilәm): you are fasting every dark moon. Its appointed duration is seven months. The deity is the Goddess Rātri Devi. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to Rātri Devi!” [I take] leave. Its result is that you will be loved by mankind; God and Demon will love you.
 [.13]There is the observance of ‘the Abundance’  (Narasvatī): one does not eat the cooked rice [that is cooked with] abundant water. It is only the rice cone without meat, just salt. Its appointed duration is one year and seven months. The deity is Goddess Rāja Lakṣmi. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to Śri Rāja Lakṣmi!” [I take] leave. Its result is that your good qualities will come out, and all your aims will be fulfilled, and then you will be famous, and you will be in harmony with your spouse. 
 [.14]There is the observance of ‘Always in Bloom’  (Muṣpasadā): when one should eat the flower as meat first. Its appointed duration is one year. The deity is Reverend Smara. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to Smara!” [I take] leave. Its result is that one’s face is matchless; many [people] will love you deeply. 
 [.15]There is the observance of ‘Applying Salve’ (Ṅlepana): when one eats the side dish as meat first. Its appointed duration is three years. The deity is the Goddess Śrī. The mantra is: “Om̐ hail to Śrī!”. [I take] leave. Its result is your face being beautiful and calm, being rich in rice, many people will love you and have sympathy for you.

 [.453]There is the observance of eating while looking in the mirror every full moon day. Its appointed duration is one year. Its result is being reborn as a rich senior customary village head (kabayan buyut) . 
 [.454]This is the sacred text on the hair (Aji Rambut): one eats the black cooked rice every fourth day of the bright half of the lunar month. The mantra is: “I am performing the sacred teaching of the hair that is not red, not transparent, not white. May my hair be black just like a crow: long, soft , [and] thick. It is obtained presently. I am performing the sacred teaching of hair, which is good presently. Surely, I am performing the sacred teaching of hair. Om̐ Sa Ba Ta A I! Hail to Śiva!” 
 [.455]There is the observance of being silent in worship. Its result is that you will be loved by God. 
 [.456]There is the observance of being silent when defecating, urinating, taking a bath, applying oil, wearing undergarments, [and] walking. Its appointed duration is seven months. Lord Śiva gives the observance. [Alternatively, if] something goes wrong, one will be rich in property, rich in offspring, and be obeyed [by people], [as well as] being reborn as a minister. 
 [.457]In 1722 Śaka (1800 AD), on Thursday Kalivon, Vāra Marakih, on the first day of the bright lunar fortnight in the month Asuji (September-October). The unit is two. The tens are two. That is the time when it is completely written.
