colophon: iti strīsaṅgrahaṇacarita.      translation : 
 [.1]Let there be no obstacle!
 [.1](When the king is going to try a case, he should enter the court modestly accompanied by Brahmins and counselors who are experts in policy.) Now the practice of the king should be that he attends to litigation of the people. He should keep the company of Brahmins knowledgeable in studying treatises (maṅaji), along with his counselors who know how to judge with discernment. He should be dignified as he enters the court
