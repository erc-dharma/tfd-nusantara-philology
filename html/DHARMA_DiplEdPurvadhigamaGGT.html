Witness

    Griya Gede Taman Collection, Taman Sari, Badung
  • [GGT] Taman Sari, Badung, Griya Gede Taman Collection, Not supplied
    • Content:
      • 1v1–17v3: Pūrvādhigama Śāsana by Anonymous
      • 17v4–19v3: Vidhi Pipiñcatan by Anonymous
    • Physical Description: Palm-leaf manuscript. The supplied transcription covers folios 1v–19v3.
    • History: The manuscript formed part of the collection of Ida Pedanda Wayan Kekeran, listed by L.C. Heyting in 1922 (Oudheidkundig Verslag 1922: 87); the collection then comprised 84 titles. The supplied note associates the colophon with Purwamimba, Saniscara Paing, wuku Manahil, and a Śaka year expressed as rah 6 tǝnggǝk 3. These historical details have not been independently verified.
