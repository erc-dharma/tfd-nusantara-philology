    • Content:
      • Durgāstava Aṣṭakamantra
    • Colophon:
    • Physical Description: This manuscript is a lontar (ləmpiran). It is preserved at Perpustakaan Nasional Republik Indonesia, listed by Behrend (1998: page [...], № L 128). A commonly occurring circle-shaped symbol appears with two different headmarks, and the combinations are marked with the @1 or @2.
    • Hand Description:

      Main hand of the manuscript, black ink, in Javanese script of the Merapi-Merbabu type

  • [J2] Jakarta, Perpustakaan Nasional Republik Indonesia, Koleksi Naskah Nusantara, L. 876
    • Content:
      • Composite Śaiva and Buddhist Manuscript
    • Colophon:
    • Physical Description: This manuscript is a lontar (ləmpiran), measuring 51 cm in length and consisting of 98 folios with text in Old Javanese language written in Balinese script. It was described by 1911: page 326, № MDCCCXXXVIII (cod. 876). It is preserved at Perpustakaan Nasional Republik Indonesia and listed by 1998: page 370, № 71 L 876. The manuscript consists of two separately numbered parts. The first part contains exclusively Śaiva texts, whereas the second contains Buddhist texts together with material that appears to be Śaiva. This manuscript was used by 1929 for his edition of the Kalpabuddha.
    • Hand Description: Main hand of the manuscript, black ink, in Balinese script.
    • History: Transliterated by Arlo Griffiths, Ida Bagus Komang Sudarma, and I Gde Agus Darma Putra from photographs.
  • [SS]
