Witness

  • [MSS_Jav53o] London, British Library, Mss./IOL. Jav. 53o
    • Content: This is a multi-text manuscript.
      • fol. 13v-17v; 19r-23v; 25v-26v: Raṇayajña
      • fol. 5r-12v; 18r-18v: Ḍārmma kamulaniṅ dadi
      • fol. 27r; 28r; 29r: Homa Tirtha
      • fol. 24r-24v; 27v; 28v; 29v: Homa Aghni
    • Colophon:
      • diplomatic: Iti raṇayajñān, bvat śivājñāna, I śaka, nora butha marganiṅ voṅ, dina, saptavara, vr̥sti, pāñcavara, kalivon, vukir, vukunya, raṇayajñā prantuṣakəna de saṁ śudyamaca, muha saṁ makeken, lvi lvaṅana, kiraṁ vəhana.
      • translation: Thus Ranayajña, in the manner of Śivājñāna, (written) on “nora (0) butha (5) marganiṅ (5) voṁ (1)” śaka (1628 AD). In the day of seven day cycle, Vr̥sti, five day cycle kalivon, the vuku is vukir. Ranayajnya to satisfy those who want to read, the same as Saṅ Makeken(?). If it is lacking, please add, if excessive please reduce.‘’
    • Physical Description: Palm-leaf manuscript. 42 leaves in Javanese script of the Merapi-Merbabu type
    • Hand Description: A1:H1 main hand of the manuscript, black ink, Kawi script
    • History: IOL(Mss.) Jav. 53o is part of MSS. Jav. 53 collections. According to Mackenzie’s report (published in Blagden, 1916), this collection consists of 24 MSS writtten in cadjan leaves. These are said to have been brought by the civility of a regent from a long deserted house in distant forests, where they had lain neglected for many years. The information on IOL Jav. 53z suggests the regent in question was Kyahi Tumenggung Puger. These MSS thus to be collected, probably from the area of Puger in East Java (the ’East Hook’).
