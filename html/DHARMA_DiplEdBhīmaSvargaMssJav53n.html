Witness

  • [MSS_Jav53n] London, British Library, Mss./IOL. Jav. 53n
    • Colophon:
      • diplomatic: Iti bimasvaṙga samakta təlasinurat…(I)mbaṁ (da)kṣiṇna| batuṙ ravaṇnasada…dyan kahuvusan iṅ anulad, vuku pahaṁ, saptavara tumpək, pañcavara vu pon, caturvara jaya, travara byatara, sasi cetra, tr̥ṣṇapakṣa ri bavani, I saka “kr̥ti bramanā bayu tuṅgal” (1584 Ś), Om̐ śri śri yaṅ svarasati ya namaḥ
    • Physical Description: Palm-leaf manuscript. 24 leaves in Javanese script of the Merapi-Merbabu type
    • Hand Description: A1:H1 main hand of the manuscript, black ink, Kawi script
    • History: IOL(Mss.) Jav. 53n is part of MSS. Jav. 53 collections. According to Mackenzie’s report (published in Blagden, 1916), this collection consists of 24 MSS writtten in cadjan leaves. These are said to have been brought by the civility of a regent from a long deserted house in distant forests, where they had lain neglected for many years. The information on IOL Jav. 53z suggests the regent in question was Kyahi Tumenggung Puger. These MSS thus to be collected, probably from the area of Puger in East Java (the ’East Hook’).
